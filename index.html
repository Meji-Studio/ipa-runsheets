Wardrobe & essentials

Smart casual, the same on both days so footage cuts together.

Essential

Belt

Non-negotiable. Leather, brown or tan. It's what pulls the smart-casual look together on camera.

  • No shorts or trousers on camera without one
  • Skip busy buckles or logos

Polo shirt

Neutral colors only, tucked in.

  • White, sand, stone grey, or olive
  • No large logos or graphic prints

Bottoms

Tailored chino shorts or trousers in neutral tones: sand, stone, or navy.

  • Worn with the belt

Shoes

Clean white sneakers.

  • Freshly cleaned, no visible scuffs
  • Skip chunky soles or bold branding
The look: smart casual throughout. Neutral polo tucked into belted chinos or shorts, white sneakers, minimal jewelry, no baseball caps, hair neat. The palette should sit quietly against the greenery, river and natural materials of the resort, not compete with it.
  • Bring a second outfit in case of rain or sweat.
  • Construction areas may need closed safety shoes and a hard hat. Pack them for walk-throughs.
  • Share these colors with April so you don't clash on screen. Avoid thin stripes and small checks, which flicker on camera.
